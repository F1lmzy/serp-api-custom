# Google search captures

Terms for capturing, comparing and storing Google search results.

## Language

**Provider response**:
One provider's observation of a query, including its AI Overview, ordered organic results and capture outcome.

**Query occurrence**:
A query's position in an input round. Repeated query text can have separate occurrences; repeat rounds retain the occurrence's identity.

**Native capture**:
A saved provider response in the provider's original structure, with capture context and credential redaction. A custom batch capture contains the ordered responses for its planned query occurrences.

**Shared record**:
A provider response expressed in the common AIO and organic-result vocabulary, with its provenance, statuses and warnings. It retains provider and query-occurrence identity rather than merging observations.

**Response storage**:
Validation and retention of complete provider responses. Each stored response retains its provider, query occurrence and repeat-round identity, including explicit failed or unresolved outcomes.
